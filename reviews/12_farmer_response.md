# 12 — Farmer response to the panel (Ramesh Patil, Yavatmal)

*I read the CEO (01), ML researcher (03) and UI expert (04) reviews. Today is 3 October. I sow chana between mid-October and November, so whatever you build in "1–2 weeks" reaches me just in time, or not at all.*

## 1. What the experts got right about my life

- **Money first, model second.** The CEO and the ML researcher both want to rank crops by ₹ profit per acre with a "bad year" number, and to use the district's real cropping history. That one change would stop the app telling me to grow coffee. The rule "new for your area, trial plot only, can never be rank 1" (03 §3) is exactly right.
- **The glut comes from all of us planting the same thing.** The CEO calls the planting registry "the moat". He is right that nobody, not the mandi and not the government, knows what my taluka is sowing before we sow.
- **Polls with no price are useless to me.** Price, grade, pickup point and delivery window (01 §4, 04 §5) are the bare minimum.
- **MSP is not a real floor everywhere.** The ML researcher only assumes MSP where procurement actually happens. That is honest. In Vidarbha, chana and soybean procurement centres open late and fill up quickly.
- **The UI expert's list of what is wrong with the screens** is what I also felt: show the village, not numbers; ask about water; give photo tiles for soil; split the audio into chapters; no "votes".

## 2. What they got wrong, or what would not work in my village

- **The 25% cap on onion** (01 §2). As a hard lock, I would not accept it. My borewell patch is 1.5 of my 3.5 acres, and onion is one of the few crops that pays on it, so 25% of my land is less than one acre. I would accept it as a **warning with a reason**, for example: "180 acres of onion are already planned near you for March. Last time that happened, the price fell to ₹800." Do not decide for me. Show me the crowd. Better still, help me **shift my harvest weeks** instead of cutting my area.
- **Phone + 4-digit PIN** (04 §4) is better than picking your name from a list, but I will forget the PIN. My son changes SIM cards, and three of us use one phone. Use an OTP or a WhatsApp login, and let the FPO or my son register me. "Forgot PIN" must not mean losing my plan.
- **"Tell buyers" publishing my plan.** If every trader can see that I will have 10 quintal of chana in March, he will wait and push the price down. Show buyers the cluster total, not my name, until I accept their offer.
- **The UI examples are Punjab examples**: wheat, mustard, potato, bigha. Here, Rabi means chana, a little wheat, and onion. We measure land in **acre and guntha** (40 guntha = 1 acre), not bigha.
- **Forward contracts.** Here is what would make me sign:
  - **Price:** MSP or the Yavatmal APMC modal price on delivery day, whichever is higher. Or a fixed price that is at least my cost plus 20%, and if the market goes 15% above it, we share the extra.
  - **Advance:** ₹1,000–2,000 per acre at sowing, or seed and fertiliser on credit against the contract.
  - **Payment:** within 48 hours by UPI, with a late-payment penalty paid to me.
  - **Quality:** moisture and grade limits written down before sowing, measured at pickup with the FPO present, and I get to see the meter.
  - **Weather:** if rain or pest destroys my crop, I pay no penalty.
  - **Penalties both ways:** if I side-sell I pay a penalty, but the same amount applies if the buyer rejects my crop when the market falls.
- **The ₹/acre and P10 numbers.** I will trust them only if they are said the way my neighbour would say them. Never say "P10" or "₹18,240". Say: "In a normal year about ₹25,000 per acre stays in your hand after costs. In a bad year like 2023, about ₹5,000, or you could lose ₹5,000. Bad years come about 1 in 5." Name a real year I remember, round the numbers, and tell me what costs you counted (seed, fertiliser, spraying, labour, my own labour or not). Then I can check it against my own diary.

## 3. Answers to the questions you asked me

- **Water (ML):** 2 acres are rainfed only. 1.5 acres have a borewell: good until December, weak in January–February, dry by March. That is enough for 2 irrigations of chana, not 5–6 for wheat.
- **Where I sell (ML):** Yavatmal APMC, about 25 km away. Sometimes Pusad. Often the village trader, who pays cash at my door. Cotton goes to CCI if the centre is open, otherwise to a private ginning mill.
- **Storage (ML):** I can keep soybean and chana in the house for 2–3 months. I have no onion chawl, so a little onion can go under a shed for a few weeks at most.
- **Loss I can survive (ML):** ₹15,000–20,000 in one season. Beyond that I cannot repay the KCC, and the moneylender starts.
- **Fixed price below a good-year price? (CEO):** Yes, for about half my crop, if it is at least MSP. I keep the other half for the market.
- **What advance makes a promise believable? (CEO):** Real money at sowing, even ₹1,000 an acre, plus a name the village knows: an FPO or a KVK.
- **How many acres of tomato? (CEO):** None, or 10 guntha at most. I have seen it thrown on the road.
- **Acre, bigha or killa? (UI):** Acre and guntha.
- **Would I trust a ₹ number? (UI):** More if it names a real buyer near me and shows the MSP next to it.
- **Audio in chapters or one story? (UI):** Chapters, each under a minute.
- **Who uses the phone? (UI):** Me for calls and WhatsApp, my son for reading, my wife for YouTube.

## 4. My TOP 5 for v1, to use it this Rabi season

1. **Marathi from the first screen, and pick my village (or use GPS).** No coordinates and no English menus.
2. **One water question, with the land split into rainfed and borewell.** Then 3 crops that actually grow here (chana, wheat, onion, safflower), each showing ₹ per acre for a normal year and a bad year, said in words.
3. **Real prices**: the last few weeks at Yavatmal and Pusad APMC, the MSP (chana ₹5,875, wheat ₹2,585), and **when and where to register for procurement**.
4. **Short Marathi audio chapters**, saved after the first download: sowing date, variety, seed per acre, fertiliser in bags, irrigation days, and pests (pod borer in chana).
5. **At least one written buyer offer before I sow**, with price, pickup and payment terms, even if it is from only one FPO.

**What I don't care about:** the map, ERA5 and grid captions, N/P/K boxes, the Plotly chart, dark mode, fonts, vendor maps, and chat. Show me the rupees and the buyer, in Marathi, by the end of October.
